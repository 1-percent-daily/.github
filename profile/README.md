## What 1% Daily is

A goal app for people building startups on the side of a job. You set one
money goal, list the startups that will get you there, and 1% Daily keeps
score of what you actually do:

```
Today (Top 3)  →  Week (3 commitments)  →  Startup  →  Money goal
```

- **One money goal**: target, deadline, what you need per month and the pace you are really at.
- **Top 3 a day**: three priorities, each tied to a weekly commitment.
- **3 commitments a week**, a weekly score and a weekly review.
- **Your startups side by side**: stage, net profit, ROAS, and which ones got no focus this week.
- **Salary planner**: how much of your salary can safely go into your startups.
- In USD, EUR or Ariary.

## What it solves

Side projects drift when nothing measures them: busy days pass, and nothing
moves the one number that matters. 1% Daily ties every day to that number,
so small progress compounds instead of getting lost.
